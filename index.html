About Me

My name is Zhihao Li, and I grew up in Biyang / Zhumadian, a beautiful city on the southern plains of Henan Province, China.
Now I’m a first-year PhD student jointly cultivated by PC National Laboratory and Peking University. My research direction is 3D Vision and Digital Human. As a blogger, I am very excited to share with you the learning experience and technical points in the major, and discuss the tricky problems in the technical field together.
